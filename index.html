Researcher, Wireless AI Technology Research Centre

China Telecom Research Institute

Dr. Xing Wei is a researcher at the China Telecom Research Institute. He earned his Ph.D. in Signal and Information Processing from Beihang University, China, in 2025. From 2022 to 2023, he was a guest researcher at the Fraunhofer Heinrich Hertz Institute in Germany. His research focuses on immersive communications, generative AI for communications, and AI-native networks.
